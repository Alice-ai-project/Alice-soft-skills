# Pitch — Alice: Soft Skills Platform with AI

**Time:** 8 minutes | **Words:** ~1,100
**Team:** 3 people | **Audience:** Professors and business guests

---

## SLIDE 1 — Opening

**(Person 1 walks to the center. Look at the audience. Pause 2 seconds. Speak slow and clear.)**

**PERSON 1:**

Good morning. Before we start, I have a question for you. How many times did you have a good idea in a class, in a meeting, or in a job interview... but you didn't say it?

**(Pause. Look at different people in the audience.)**

Why? Maybe you were scared. Maybe you didn't know how to say it. Maybe you didn't have time.

If you raised your hand, you are not alone. If you didn't raise your hand, maybe it is difficult to say it. This is the problem. This is why we created Alice.

---

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem

**(Person 2 stands next to Person 1. Point to the data on the screen. Speak with a serious tone.)**

**PERSON 2:**

In RIWI, we work with students every day. We see a big problem.

**(Point to the slide with data)**

Studies from Harvard, Stanford, and the Carnegie Foundation say this: **85% of professional success** depends on soft skills. Only **15% depends on technical knowledge**. But schools and universities spend **100% of the time** teaching technical subjects.

**(Pause for 2 seconds)**

The result? Professionals who know how to code, how to do accounting, how to practice law... but they don't know how to explain an idea clearly. They don't know how to handle stress in a negotiation. They don't know how to lead a team. They don't know how to manage their time.

In RIWI, we see this every day. Students who don't want to participate in class. Students who are afraid to speak in public. Students who procrastinate. They don't have tools to change. This is not a problem of intelligence. This is a problem of skills that nobody taught them.

---

## SLIDE 3 — The Solution: Alice

**(Person 3 walks to the stage. Turn on the platform presentation. Speak with energy but calm.)**

**PERSON 3:**

Alice is the answer. Alice is a web platform to diagnose, train, and develop soft skills in schools and companies.

**(Point to the D-ID avatar on the screen)**

And this is what makes us different. Alice is not a PDF. Alice is not a simple chatbot. Alice has an AI assistant with a face, a voice, and a presence. We use D-ID technology for this. It is a practice partner. It simulates real conversations. It evaluates how you speak. It gives you feedback in real time.

**(Point to the avatar)**

Do you want to practice a presentation before you speak to a committee? Alice listens to you. Alice analyzes your speech. Alice tells you what to improve. Do you need to prepare for a job negotiation? Alice simulates the situation with you. The avatar has facial expressions. It feels human.

**(Pause for the audience to see the demo)**

But Alice is not only a conversation. Alice starts with a **diagnostic based on Daniel Goleman's model**. Goleman is the international standard for emotional intelligence. We evaluate six dimensions: self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills, and emotional connection.

**(Show the courses on screen)**

After the diagnostic, the platform creates a **personalized learning roadmap** with 11 specialized courses: assertive communication, empathetic leadership, time management, conflict resolution, frustration resistance, and more.

Each user receives a different plan. There is no generic content. There is no one path for everyone. Alice learns from you. Alice guides you to the path you really need.

---

## SLIDE 4 — Business Model

**(Person 2 takes the focus again. Show the business model and numbers. Speak slow and strategic.)**

**PERSON 2:**

Now, let's talk about business. Why is this a viable and scalable business?

The global corporate training market is more than **370 billion dollars per year**. Companies invest a lot of money in talent development programs. But most of these investments don't have objective measurement. They hire consultants. They pay for workshops. They send employees to conferences. At the end of the year, nobody can say if these investments really improved the team's skills.

**(Pause)**

Alice solves this problem. Our business model has two parts: B2B and B2C.

- **For companies and HR departments:** We offer a SaaS solution. It diagnoses socio-emotional competencies with quantitative data. It creates personalized training plans at scale. It measures the real impact of training with data, not with perceptions.

- **For universities and educational centers:** Alice works as a complement to professional careers. It gives students a competitive advantage before they enter the job market. Imagine: every graduate from this faculty starts their first job with communication, leadership, and emotional management skills already developed and certified.

- **For coaches, organizational psychologists, and consulting firms:** A digital tool that improves their services. Automated diagnostic. Continuous practice with AI.

The scalability is in the architecture. We use Docker. It works with any existing infrastructure. The implementation takes days, not months.

---

## SLIDE 5 — Competitive Advantage

**(Person 1 returns to the center. Show the comparison table with Coursera/Udemy. Speak with confidence.)**

**PERSON 1:**

Why not Coursera? Why not Udemy? Why not LinkedIn Learning?

**(Point to the comparison table)**

Because those platforms give you **content**. Alice gives you **experience**.

Coursera offers you a 20-hour recorded course about leadership. You watch it. You pass a quiz. You receive a PDF certificate. But when you go to a real meeting and you need to solve a conflict between two team members... what do you do with that certificate?

Alice is different. Alice works on four levels that traditional platforms don't cover:

**First:** Diagnostic based on scientific evidence. Not generic questionnaires from the internet.

**Second:** Structured educational content with 11 specialized courses. Each one has theory, downloadable resources, and assessments.

**Third:** Interactive practice with an AI assistant with visual presence through D-ID. It simulates real situations of communication and leadership.

**Fourth:** A tracking dashboard. Institutions and companies can measure each individual's progress with concrete data.

**(Pause)**

This is not an online course. This is a **competency transformation platform**.

---

## SLIDE 6 — Closing and Call to Action

**(The three team members stand together. Look at the audience. Speak with emotion and clarity.)**

**PERSON 3:**

Let's go back to the beginning. How many times did you have a good idea and you didn't say it?

**PERSON 2:**

You don't overcome that fear reading a book. You overcome it practicing. You overcome it receiving feedback. You overcome it repeating until it becomes instinct.

**PERSON 1:**

Alice is not another educational product. Alice is the tool that connects human potential with the competencies that the job market really demands. And it is ready.

**(Pause 3 seconds. Look at the audience calmly.)**

**PERSON 3:**

We don't ask you to believe in an idea. We ask you to believe in what you are seeing. Alice works. The technology is integrated. The diagnostic is validated. The 11 courses are developed.

**PERSON 2:**

What we need now is the next step. Implement this platform in real learning environments. Show with data that soft skills can be taught, measured, and developed systematically.

**PERSON 1:**

We invite you to be part of this process. Join us in validating a model that can change the way institutions train people.

**(The three stand in silence for 2 seconds. Confident smile.)**

**PERSON 3:**

Thank you very much.

**(Clean cut. The D-ID avatar image stays on screen as the final visual.)**

## Notes for the Team

- **Speaking speed:** Approximately 130-150 words per minute for a natural and calm pace.
- **Transitions:** Each change of person should include a pause of 1-2 seconds.
- **Eye contact:** Alternate between three sections of the audience (left, center, right).
- **D-ID Avatar:** Keep the avatar demo active during slides 3 and 5.
- **Important:** Practice pronunciation of key words: "soft skills", "diagnostic", "scalable", "competency", "infrastructure".
- **Avoid:** Reading the slides directly. This script is for practice, not for reading live.
